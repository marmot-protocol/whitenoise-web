# Send a message

Both people need White Noise, or another compatible Marmot client, set up and connected. A Nostr public key identifies the person; receiving an invitation also requires their client to publish invitation keys.

Find the person using their Nostr public key or profile QR code, then start a direct chat.

[<img src="/docs/android/chats.png" alt="White Noise Android Chats screen" loading="lazy" width="1080" height="2400" />](/docs/android/chats.png)

## Find the person

1. On **Chats**, tap the pencil control labelled **New Message** to open the recipient search.
2. In **Name or npub**, type a name or Nostr address, or tap **Paste** to insert a copied npub or profile link. To scan a profile code instead, tap **Connect with QR**.
3. Choose the person from the results, where people you follow are marked “You follow”.

[<img src="/docs/android/new-message.png" alt="White Noise Android New Message screen" loading="lazy" width="1080" height="2400" />](/docs/android/new-message.png)

If White Noise marks an address as unverified, tap **Retry** or confirm the address with the person before you message them. If the network search fails or looks incomplete, check your connection and tap **Retry**; pasting their npub directly also works.

## Send your first message

1. Open the chat and wait while White Noise sets up encryption and shows an empty conversation.
2. Type your message in the composer and send it.

**Expected result:** your text appears in the chat. A local send status reports progress on your device only, and the recipient may still be offline or yet to read it.

[<img src="/docs/android/conversation.png" alt="White Noise Android Conversation screen" loading="lazy" width="1080" height="2400" />](/docs/android/conversation.png)

If starting the chat fails, check your connection and tap **Retry**. If White Noise shows **Chat created** with **Open chat**, the chat exists and is still reaching your list, so wait a moment and tap **Open chat**. If a message shows **Send failed**, check your connection and see [Delivery problems](/docs/troubleshooting/delivery).

## Let others message you

In **Settings**, tap your profile card (**Share & Connect**) to show your QR code or copy your npub for others.

## Invite someone new

Tap **Invite a friend** to open your device's sharing options with a link to download White Noise.

## Accept or decline an invitation

When someone starts a chat or group with you, it appears as an invitation in your chat list. Open it and tap **Accept** to join or **Decline** to turn it down. If the invitation is still being checked, wait for it to finish, and if checking fails, tap **Retry**. See [Invitation problems](/docs/troubleshooting/invitations) for more help.
