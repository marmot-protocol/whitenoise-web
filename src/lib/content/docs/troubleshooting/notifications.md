# Notifications

**Outcome:** New messages trigger an alert while White Noise runs in the background.

Notifications are alerts only. A missing alert does not mean a message failed, and an alert does not prove delivery.

## Diagnose

1. Open White Noise. If new messages appear, delivery works and only the alerts need fixing.
2. Make sure your operating system allows notifications for White Noise. You manage this permission in your device's system settings.
3. Check background conditions. Battery saving, data saving, focus modes and background app restrictions can delay or hide alerts.
4. Make sure the device stays connected in the background. VPNs and restrictive networks can block the services that alerts depend on.
5. Push notifications have their own data flows, which the privacy policy describes. Read it before you turn them on.
6. Keep your installation. Reinstalling to fix alerts can delete the conversations stored on the device.

## Expected result

With the app closed, a test message from a contact produces an alert, and the message is there when you open the app.

## Escalation

Report the app version, platform, operating system version and any settings you changed. Hide message previews and contact names in screenshots.
