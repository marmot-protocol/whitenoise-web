# Contacts

Contacts help you find people and start conversations with them. You can also keep a private nickname and notes for anyone you talk to.

## Find someone

1. Open **New Message** and, in **Name or npub**, enter a name or an identifier such as an npub or a Nostr address. Tap **Paste** to drop in a key or link you copied.
2. To use a code instead, tap **Connect with QR** and scan the person's profile QR code.
3. Pick the person from the results.

Results appear in four groups: **People in chats**, **Following**, **Network results**, and **On this device**. A **You follow** tag marks people you already follow. An address tagged **Address not verified** is still waiting for White Noise to confirm it, and if that check fails, tap **Retry** to run it again.

To bring in someone new, tap **Invite a friend** and share the invitation. It includes the download link.

## Use a person's profile

The **User Profile** screen shows the person's name, pictures, any **Verified Nostr address**, and Lightning address. Tap **Message** to chat or **Copy public key** to copy their key. Under **Profile Actions**:

- **Add Contact** follows the person, and **Remove Contact** unfollows them.
- **Start group** creates a group with them. **Add to a group** lists the groups you administer that they can still join; select groups and tap **Add to groups**.
- **Groups in Common** lists the groups you share.
- **Make admin in groups** promotes them in groups you administer where they are already a member.
- **Block** and **Unblock** switch blocking on or off for this person.

## Add a nickname and notes

1. Tap **Nickname & notes**.
2. Enter a **Name** (up to 80 characters) and **Notes**, then tap **Save**.
3. To set a private picture, tap **Pick photo** under **Private picture**, then **Reposition / crop** if needed.

Nicknames and notes stay on this device, visible only to you. The private picture applies only to this account on this device and is never uploaded. Clear the nickname to show the profile name again, or tap **Clear private picture** to go back to their public photo.

## Set notifications for one person

Tap **Notifications**. Choose **Use defaults** or **Mute notifications from this person**, or fine-tune **Sound & system behavior** and **Vibration pattern**. The setting covers messages from this person in every conversation for this account, while delivery and unread counts stay the same. Chat mute and mentions-only rules apply first.

## Fix common problems

- If a network search fails, check your connection and tap **Retry**.
- If White Noise rejects a recipient as invalid, enter an npub, a profile link, or a hex public key instead.
- A block can save on your device before the network confirms it. Repeat the same block or unblock action to finish it.
- If following or unfollowing fails to update, reconnect and try again.
- When **Groups in Common** reports that some memberships are unavailable, tap **Retry** on that screen.
