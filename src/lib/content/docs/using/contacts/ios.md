# Contacts

On iOS you find people and start conversations from the chat list. Search or scan a profile QR code to reach people already on White Noise, and send an invitation to everyone else.

## Start a chat with someone

1. In the chat list, tap **New Message** to open the **New Chat** screen.
2. Search for the person, or tap **Scan QR Code** and scan their profile code, then select them.
3. If you already have a conversation with this person, White Noise asks which one to continue. Tap it, or tap **Start new conversation**.

Expected result: the conversation opens. When you search by Nostr address, a **Verified** badge marks an address White Noise has confirmed.

## Invite someone

On the **New Chat** screen, tap **Invite to White Noise**. Then tap **Share Invite** and choose how to send it.

## Start a group

1. On the **New Chat** screen, tap **New Group**.
2. Select people. They appear under **Selected**.
3. Tap **Continue** to open **Set Up Group**.

A group that starts with just you needs a name. Unnamed groups display their members' names instead, and you can add members after creating the group.

## Help others find you

Open **Share & Connect** to share your profile URL or picture, or to show your QR code. See [Your profile on iOS](/docs/using/profiles/ios).

## Fix common problems

- If an address search comes up empty, check the spelling or ask the person to share their profile URL or QR code.
- If White Noise reports that it couldn't check an address, check your connection and tap **Retry**.
- If a chat fails to start, tap **Retry**.
- When a chat shows as unavailable, it may still be syncing. Wait a moment, then tap **Retry**.
