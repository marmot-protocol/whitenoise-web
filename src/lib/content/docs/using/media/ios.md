# Media and downloads

Send photos, videos, voice messages, and GIFs, view what you receive, and control downloads and storage.

## Before you share

- Identifying photo metadata, such as location, is always removed before sending. Check videos and files for embedded details before you send them.
- GIF search sends your search and IP address to GIPHY. Opening a received GIF also contacts GIPHY.
- Sharing media to another app gives that app the content you choose.

## Take a photo or video

1. Open the camera from the message composer.
2. Tap for a photo, or hold for video. Video includes sound when microphone access is allowed. Otherwise, tap **Videos record without sound** to open Settings and allow microphone access. **Switch Camera** changes cameras, and **Close Camera** leaves the camera.
3. The capture appears in your draft. Tap it to preview it, or tap **Remove attachment** to drop it. Then send.

If you see **Camera Access Is Off**, tap **Open Settings**, allow camera access for White Noise, and return.

## Add a GIF or other attachment

Use the composer's attachment controls. In GIF search (**Powered by** GIPHY), the privacy notice above appears before you search. **Cancel** closes the search. Attachments wait in your draft until you send. Tap **Remove attachment** to drop one.

## Record a voice message

1. Touch and hold **Voice message**. Recording starts after a short hold. A quick tap doesn't start recording.
2. Release to add the recording to your draft. For a longer recording, drag up while holding to lock it, then tap **Finish recording** when done.
3. Review the draft, then send. **Cancel recording** discards a recording in progress. **Remove attachment** drops a finished recording.

## View, play, and save media

- Tap media in a message to open it full screen. Use **Save**, **Go to Message**, or **More** for other actions.
- Videos and voice messages have a **Playback speed** control.
- For files, tap **Open attachment**.
- If media doesn't load, tap **Retry**.

If your message shows **Not delivered. Tap for options.**, tap it and choose **Try Again**. **Delete** discards the unsent message.

## Manage a single download

Open **Message info** for the message. Under **Downloads**:

- **Cancel download** stops a download in progress.
- **Download again** retries it.
- **Remove download** deletes the downloaded file from this device. It doesn't affect anyone else's copy.

## Data Usage settings

Open **Settings**, then **Data Usage**.

- **Auto-Download**: choose which media downloads automatically. Other media shows a download button in the conversation. **Reset Auto-Download Settings** restores the defaults.
- **Remote Media**: when this is on, opening a conversation can tell GIPHY your IP address and which GIF was requested. When it's off, received GIFs load only when you tap them.
- **Sent Media**: opens **Sent Media Quality**. Under **Photos and Videos**, higher quality uses more data.

## Download storage

On the **Download Storage** screen, set limits for this profile and tap **Save**. When storage is full, downloads pause and existing files are kept. If the screen doesn't load, tap **Retry**.
